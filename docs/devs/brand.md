# The Rhombus Brand

## Name

Somewhat counterintuitively, Rhombus's logo isn't inspired by the name "Rhombus", it's the other way around.
"Rhombus" is a play on the Greek letter Rho (𝝆), which is often used in physics as a symbol for the density of materials. (*density*, get it? density~ function?)
So the idea for the logo came first, and the name was based on that. There is no other specific reason.

## Logo

The Rhombus Logo is a bold italic and blue-violet colored rho in the Computer Modern font and a blue bay outlined mountain in the background.
